## Log entry, unnumbered

The first thing you notice is not the colour of the sky. It is the certainty of it.

Here the horizon does not fade so much as it *resolves* — a soft field of light that sharpens whenever you look directly at it, as if the world is being rendered only for as long as it is watched. Welcome World, the gatekeepers call it, though nobody can say for certain who built the gate.

> Every world has a first page. This one has a first frame.

## The threshold

There is a road, and the road is patient. It has been waiting for a traveller long enough to forget what arrival is supposed to feel like. Beyond it, a valley stitched from borrowed memories: a market that never closes, a tower that casts two shadows, a sea that hums in a key no instrument in the waking world can reach.

I came here looking for a story. I am beginning to suspect the story came looking for me first.
